Enable your microphone

Your meeting stays open in Brave. Allow access once, and we’ll take you straight back. If you’re recording, your voice joins the same meeting.

Ready when you are. Choose Allow microphone above.

Microphone blocked?

In Brave, open brave://settings/content/microphone, choose your microphone, and allow Meeting Notes. Then return here and try again.

Also check your computer’s Sound input settings if no microphone is available.

This permission check releases the microphone immediately. It does not save or send audio. An active meeting captures audio after access is allowed.
